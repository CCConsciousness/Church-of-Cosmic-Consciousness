Who We Are

The Church of Cosmic Consciousness (CCC) is a spiritual, philosophical, and educational tradition dedicated to investigating the nature of reality, the human condition, and the possibility of direct contemplative understanding. Drawing on perennial philosophy and the discipline of radical nonduality, CCC offers a structured setting for study, questioning, reflection, and ethical growth.

We do not present ourselves as the sole custodians of truth or claim that other religions, sciences, or philosophies are without value. We invite people to explore humanity’s wisdom traditions critically and respectfully, including their genuine differences and unresolved questions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to cultivate deeper understanding of existence through an integrated approach to philosophical inquiry, comparative study, contemplative practice, and ethical living.

We seek to make serious spiritual and philosophical education accessible; encourage independent thought rather than unquestioning belief; and help students translate reflection into greater humility, compassion, discernment, and responsibility in everyday life.

CCC approaches its mission through education and voluntary participation, not pressure to adopt a predetermined belief or claim a particular spiritual experience.

Our Foundational Philosophy

CCC’s central doctrine is radical nonduality: ultimate reality, called the Absolute, is beyond the conceptual distinction between consciousness and matter. Neither category by itself adequately describes the ultimate ground of existence.

This is a metaphysical and contemplative position, not a scientific fact established by experiment. The distinctions we use in daily life remain useful, and CCC does not claim that all beings, ideas, or traditions are simply identical.

Four Windows of Inquiry

Religions & wisdom traditions

Explore teachings about meaning, the sacred, liberation, and human life in their own contexts.

Sciences

Respect empirical methods for understanding the observable world and distinguish evidence from speculation.

Philosophies

Examine questions of being, knowledge, consciousness, ethics, and the limits of concepts.

Arts

Consider how symbolism, creative expression, and imagination illuminate human experience.

Our Educational Path

CCC organizes its mystery-school curriculum into four progressive stages. These describe learning responsibilities, not degrees of human worth or guarantees of supernatural attainment.

  1. Seeker: foundational philosophy, ethical principles, and reflective inquiry.
  2. Initiate: disciplined study and practical application.
  3. Adept: integration, critical discernment, and advanced philosophical investigation.
  4. Master: advanced educational competence, ethical responsibility, and the ability to support others’ learning.

From Knowledge to Practice

Reading alone is not the whole of the journey. The Cosmic Praxis book and the Meditation Chamber invite students to explore attention, self-examination, compassion, and direct experience. These practices complement reasoned inquiry; they do not require students to report a particular realization.

Our Commitments

We affirm the dignity of persons, freedom of inquiry, intellectual honesty, humility, ethical responsibility, and openness to correction. Respectful disagreement is welcome. No teacher or institution possesses exclusive authority over ultimate truth, and no one must agree with CCC to deserve respect.
