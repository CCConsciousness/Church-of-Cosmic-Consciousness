Meet Anton

Anton is a curious ant with six feet, an empty wallet, and a habit of stopping to ask questions. At the creek, a beetle needs a steady foot on a wobbly bridge. Anton has somewhere to be. You help him decide what to do.

Character is the pattern in how you treat others and what you do when a choice costs you something.

Defining Our Definition of Character

Character development means noticing that pattern and practicing the qualities you want to grow. Each time you keep a promise or make a mistake right, someone learns they can count on you.
