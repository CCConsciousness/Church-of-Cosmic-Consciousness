The Northern Sky

Illustrated territory: Northwest sky · conceptual artwork, not a surveyed map

Territory and history

The northern sky provided a stable celestial reference. Circumpolar stars never set at Giza’s latitude and were associated with enduring existence in Egyptian funerary traditions.

Architecture and function

The Great Pyramid is closely aligned to the cardinal directions. Stellar observations are one proposed method of establishing north; the exact surveying procedure remains uncertain.

Relationship to the whole

Compare the northern sky with the Orion chambers: the former concerns orientation and enduring stars, the latter the seasonal celestial cycle.

Symbolic reflection

Contemplate the contrast between apparent permanence and change.
