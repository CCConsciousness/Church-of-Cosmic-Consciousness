Ra and the Solar Horizon

Illustrated territory: Northeast sky · conceptual artwork, not a surveyed map

Territory and history

Ra was a major Egyptian solar deity. Solar theology connected the daily journey of the sun with creation, kingship and renewal.

Architecture and function

The eastern horizon marks sunrise, while the western horizon marks sunset. Many Egyptian mortuary landscapes drew meaning from this cycle; Giza’s complexes also had practical architectural arrangements.

Relationship to the whole

Connect this chamber with the eastern valley approaches and the western necropolis.

Symbolic reflection

The sunrise can represent illumination and renewed awareness.
