Territory and history

The Great Sphinx is a monumental limestone sculpture with a lion’s body and human head, carved largely from the bedrock of the Giza plateau.

Architecture and function

It stands near Khafre’s valley temple and is generally dated to the Old Kingdom. Its exact original meaning is debated; later Egyptians associated it with Horemakhet, Horus in the Horizon.

Relationship to the whole

Connect its guardian imagery with the solar horizon and Khafre’s complex. The Book Club below is a modern digital chamber, not a claim of a historical library beneath the monument.

Symbolic reflection

The union of human and lion forms invites reflection on intelligence and strength.
