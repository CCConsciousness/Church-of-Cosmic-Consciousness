Orion — The Three Stars

Illustrated territory: Orion east · conceptual artwork, not a surveyed map

Territory and history

The three belt stars appear close together from Earth but lie at different distances in space.

Architecture and function

The modern Orion correlation hypothesis proposes that the Giza pyramids reproduce the belt’s pattern. This is disputed and is not established as the builders’ intention. The beams in the artwork are artistic symbolism, not documented ancient machinery.

Relationship to the whole

Compare the three actual royal pyramids in the Temple and side chambers, rather than treating the artwork as a surveyed map.

Symbolic reflection

Consider the correspondence between terrestrial form and celestial imagery as a metaphor.
