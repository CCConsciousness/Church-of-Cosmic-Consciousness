Meet Bhagwan Ra Afrika

Bhagwan Ra Afrika is a spiritual teacher, mystic, and author who founded the House of Ra along with Bilal (Bilal Hazur).

Background & Teachings

Radha Soami Initiation

Initiated into the Radha Soami tradition in 1970 by Huzur Maharaji Charan Singh (Maharaj Charan Singh Ji).

House of Ra

Formal teaching began in 1989, establishing the House of Ra as a spiritual movement focused on transcending the ego, self-realization, and divine consciousness.

Themes

His discourses and writings address the anatomy of the ego, meditation and spiritual preparation, the stages of the bardo and the dying process, and spiritual traditions across African, Kemetic, Trika, and mystical lineages.
