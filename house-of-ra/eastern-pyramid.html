The Smaller Royal Pyramids — Menkaure

Illustrated territory: Eastern pyramid · conceptual artwork, not a surveyed map

Territory and history

Menkaure’s pyramid is the smallest of Giza’s three principal royal pyramids. Granite was used in portions of its casing and associated architecture.

Architecture and function

Its complex includes temples, a causeway and nearby subsidiary pyramids. Differences in size and materials show that the complexes were not identical machines.

Relationship to the whole

Compare its construction with Khufu and Khafre. The artwork’s right-hand placement is illustrative rather than a geographic identification.

Symbolic reflection

Scale need not determine symbolic significance.
