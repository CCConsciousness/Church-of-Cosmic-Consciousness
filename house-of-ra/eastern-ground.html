Causeways and Valley Temples

Illustrated territory: Eastern ground · conceptual artwork, not a surveyed map

Territory and history

Royal pyramid complexes typically linked a valley temple to a mortuary temple by a causeway. These formed part of a broader funerary landscape near the Nile floodplain.

Architecture and function

Waterways and transport infrastructure helped move supplies and stone. The precise uses of individual ritual spaces varied and are not fully known.

Relationship to the whole

Follow the conceptual route from the valley approach to the Temple and compare the Sphinx’s location near Khafre’s valley complex.

Symbolic reflection

The journey from valley to plateau can be read symbolically as a passage.
