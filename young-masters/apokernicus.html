About the developer

Appakernicus

Appakernicus is a writer, artist, and mentor from Bolingbrook, Illinois. He founded the Green Rose Organization to help people examine how they think, how they meet a difficult feeling, and how they treat the next person who crosses their path.

His curiosity runs from alchemical manuscripts to the affairs of a nearly penniless ant. In Young Master’s Board Games, those interests share a home. A gleaming stone invites you to look closer. A beetle at a creek asks for a hand. Just when you think you’ve found the moral, Anton offers a philosophical riddle.

The name, in three pieces

Appakernicus gives his name a wordplay of his own.

Appa
Above and beyond. Also the fellow who designs the apps.
Kerni
The kernel of truth. A truth speaker looks for that little seed and says what he finds.
Cus
To kiss, or to cuss out. “Which one?” you ask. That depends on the conversation.

Look for the green rose. It’s the developer’s signature. What you do when you reach the creek is yours.

A Personal Vow of Silence

"In the tradition of sacred wisdom, I embrace the silence of Thoth, Hermes and Imhotep—I AM Hermes Trismegistus, the “Thrice Great”, guardian of all knowledge and cosmic balance. This vow is not absence, but presence—an inward journey of attunement with the higher order. I hold silence as my sacred right, a space where words yield to understanding. In honoring this path, I ask for your respect, knowing that in silence, deeper truths may be heard."

"In alignment with the First Amendment of the United States Constitution, which guarantees freedom of speech, I likewise embrace the freedom not to speak. My vow of silence is an expression of spiritual autonomy, a sacred choice to listen rather than to utter. As silence is a form of self-governance, I exercise my right to inward reflection and reverence, knowing that within this quietude lies profound liberty. I ask for understanding and respect as I walk this path."
