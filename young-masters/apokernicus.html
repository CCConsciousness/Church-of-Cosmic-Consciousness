About the developer

Appakernicus

Appakernicus is a writer, artist, and mentor from Bolingbrook, Illinois. He founded the Green Rose Organization to help people examine how they think, how they meet a difficult feeling, and how they treat the next person who crosses their path.

His curiosity runs from alchemical manuscripts to the affairs of a nearly penniless ant. In Young Master’s Board Games, those interests share a home. A gleaming stone invites you to look closer. A beetle at a creek asks for a hand. Just when you think you’ve found the moral, Anton passes his hat.

The name, in three pieces

Appakernicus gives his name a wordplay of his own.

Appa
Above and beyond. Also the fellow who designs the apps.
Kerni
The kernel of truth. A truth speaker looks for that little seed and says what he finds.
Cus
To kiss, or to cuss out. “Which one?” you ask. That depends on the compensation.

Look for the green rose. It’s the developer’s signature. What you do when you reach the creek is yours.
